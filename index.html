About the conference

Across disciplines.
Across perspectives.

The conference brings scholarship into dialogue with activism, the arts and professional practice. It creates space to examine how gender and sexuality shape human experience, institutions and possibilities for change.

Read about the conference
01

Bodies and identities

How bodies, gender and sexuality are lived, represented and understood.

02

Theory and critique

Feminist, queer, trans and decolonial approaches to knowledge.

03

Institutions and power

The relationships between social structures, policy and everyday life.

04

Inequalities and violence

Critical conversations about exclusion, justice and resistance.

05

Histories and geographies

Perspectives across cultures, places, languages and time.

06

Emerging conversations

New questions and interdisciplinary approaches to gender and sexuality.

These areas reflect the conference’s established focus. The 2027 themes and call for contributions will be announced.
