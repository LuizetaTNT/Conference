Our academic community

The Gender and Sexualities Conference is organised by the Department of Gender and Sexualities within the Faculty for Social Wellbeing at the University of Malta.

The department provides the academic setting for the conference and its connection with Astarte: Mediterranean Journal on Gender and Sexualities.
