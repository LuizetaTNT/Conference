Gender and Sexualities Conference · 2027

About

An interdisciplinary space for thinking critically about gender, sexuality and the possibilities for social change.

The conference

The Gender and Sexualities Conference is organised by the Department of Gender and Sexualities, Faculty for Social Wellbeing, University of Malta, in collaboration with the Human Rights Directorate.

Bringing together researchers, artists, activists and practitioners, the conference explores gender and sexuality through social, political, historical and cultural perspectives. It welcomes critical dialogue across disciplinary boundaries.

The 2027 edition

Conference dates, venue and the call for contributions will be announced here.

Areas of dialogue

The following areas reflect the conference’s established focus. The final 2027 themes will be confirmed with the call for contributions.

01

Bodies and identities

How bodies, gender and sexuality are lived, represented and understood.

02

Theory and critique

Feminist, queer, trans and decolonial approaches to knowledge.

03

Institutions and power

The relationships between social structures, policy and everyday life.

04

Inequalities and violence

Critical conversations about exclusion, justice and resistance.

05

Histories and geographies

Perspectives across cultures, places, languages and time.

06

Emerging conversations

New questions and interdisciplinary approaches to gender and sexuality.

In dialogue with Astarte

The conference is connected with Astarte: Mediterranean Journal on Gender and Sexualities, sharing an interest in interdisciplinary scholarship and conversations across cultures and histories.
